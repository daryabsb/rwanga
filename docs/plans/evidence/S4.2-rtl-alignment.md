### RTL-09: the "known bug" cross-reference is **STALE** — corrected

The checklist row carried *"Known bug: real slug sits in an action block"* (via SW-08). That is not
what this document does. The fixture holds **47 `sceneHeading` nodes for 47 scenes**, and they render
as `.rga-print-block-sceneHeading` — bold, flush to the reading-start edge, carrying the scene number
and the brand-pink recognition underline. The rendered slug reads `١ ناوەوە ژووری نالی — رۆژ`, i.e.
the **localized** `INT.` (`ناوەوە`) is in place. The note is removed when the row flips.
*Scope honesty:* this proves the **render** path over a real document. The **typing** path (does a
newly typed slug land in a sceneHeading block?) is covered by the script-framework tests, not by this
sweep — the two are different claims and this evidence only carries the first.

### Observations recorded, not failed

- **Transitions are still the English `CUT`**, not the localized `قطع` / Kurdish equivalent. Per the
  protocol this is a **vocabulary** matter, not geometry — RTL-08 passes on alignment. Carried to
  **SW-23** (S4.5), which owns "the profile drives the convention", vocabulary included.
- **Eastern-Arabic numerals** (`٣`, `٨`) render throughout, while the convention's default is
  Western-Arabic with Eastern as a locale option. The document's own content chose them, so this is
  correct behaviour; it is carried to SW-23 only to confirm the locale setting drives it.
- **GAP-3-4's two RTL reds** (`scene-navigator-rtl-expansion.spec.js`) are **not** part of this
  surface — they concern the Scene Navigator sidebar's mark indentation, and both the RTL test and
  its LTR control report zero indent, which points at the marks having lost their indent entirely
  rather than at a direction bug. Left in GAP-3-4 for the Scene Navigator, not folded here: this
  sweep found the screenplay page itself correct, so mixing the two would muddy both verdicts.
